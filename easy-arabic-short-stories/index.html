Free audio · Level A1

Easy Arabic Short Stories for Beginners

قِصَصٌ عَرَبِيَّةٌ قَصِيرَةٌ

Every story read slowly in Modern Standard Arabic by the author: the vocabulary first, then the whole story, then the story again at normal speed.

How to use the audio

  1. Open your book at the same story and follow the Arabic text while you listen.
  2. After each word and sentence there is a short pause: say it out loud (“shadowing”).
  3. Too fast? Choose 0.75× below. When it feels easy, go back to 1×.
