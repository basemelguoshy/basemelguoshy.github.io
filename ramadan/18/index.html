Dua of the Day

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ، تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللَّهِ، وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّه

“In the name of Allah, I trust in Allah, and there is no power or strength except with Allah.”

Said when leaving home - Abu Dawud 5095
