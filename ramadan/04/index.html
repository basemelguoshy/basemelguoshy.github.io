Dua of the Day

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَطْعَمَنِي هَذَا وَرَزَقَنِيهِ مِنْ غَيْرِ حَوْلٍ مِنِّي وَلَا قُوَّة

Alhamdu lillahil-ladhi at'amani hadha wa razaqanihi min ghayri hawlin minni wa la quwwah

“All praise is for Allah who fed me this and gave it to me without any power or strength from me.”

Said after eating - Abu Dawud 4023, Tirmidhi 3458
